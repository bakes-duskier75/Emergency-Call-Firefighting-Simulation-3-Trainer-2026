## 🎮 About the Game

Emergency Call - The Firefighting Simulation 3 is an action and simulation game developed by Crenetic GmbH Studios and published by Aerosoft. It entered Early Access on August 5, 2026. Players serve in a German professional fire department, complete 24-hour shifts, train at the station, respond to emergencies, and qualify for roles ranging from attack crew member to emergency vehicle engineer.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Emergency Call - The Firefighting Simulation 3**. This trainer supports the game's progression, station activities, physical condition, equipment handling, and emergency operations. It is designed for single-player use and helps reduce repetitive preparation while preserving the core firefighting loop of training, driving to incidents, using equipment, and managing dynamic fires.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the fire station, training system, emergency vehicles, missions, and firefighting mechanics with less grinding or reduced difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the fire station and operational area normally. Use the trainer's stamina option when running, climbing, carrying equipment, or working under pressure. Vehicle driving remains part of the normal gameplay loop, including responding to incidents across the operational area.

### Tools & Inventory

Equipment is handled through the game's interaction system. Common tools include hoses, the Halligan tool, thermal imaging equipment, and other firefighting gear. Activate trainer options before an exercise or mission if you want to reduce equipment-use limitations.

### World & Environment

The fire station functions as the main hub between calls. Complete training courses, drills, fitness sessions, station routines, vehicle preparation, and shift activities before responding to incidents. The operational area includes varied urban districts, changing weather, a day-night cycle, rescue work, fires, traffic accidents, recovery tasks, and hazard protection missions.

### Combat & Weapons

The game has no conventional combat system. Emergency response is based on firefighting and rescue procedures. Use the trainer to manage stamina, qualifications, and progression while handling fires, operating hoses, using tools, and completing incident objectives.
